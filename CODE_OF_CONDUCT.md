# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and research-oriented community.
Examples of positive participation include:

- discussing technical disagreements with evidence and patience;
- welcoming contributors with different experience levels;
- giving credit for data, research, code, and documentation;
- protecting credentials, private data, and unpublished research material;
- accepting maintainer decisions about project scope and reproducibility.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, deliberate credential exposure,
misrepresentation of data provenance, and repeated disruptive behavior are not
acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions,
reviews, and other public spaces representing the project.

## Enforcement

The repository maintainer may edit or remove contributions that violate this
standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

For ordinary technical disagreements, use the relevant Issue or Pull Request.
For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through a private
contact method listed on the
[`@wcc961129` GitHub profile](https://github.com/wcc961129). Do not disclose
personal information or credentials in a public Issue.

Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
